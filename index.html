WHY DID I GET THIS?

Not just "this is a scam" — here's the trick being played on you

Every scam message is built the same way: pressure, then a way out. ScamSathi names the pressure so it stops working.

"Dear customer, your account will be BLOCKED within 24 hours due to KYC mismatch. Update immediately: bit.ly/kyc-verify-now"
⏳
UrgencyA deadline ("24 hours") removes your time to verify anything.
⚠️
Fear"Blocked" threatens something you rely on — your own money.
🏛️
Authority impersonationSounds official, so you skip the "would my bank actually text this?" question.
Scammers don't need you to be careless. They need you to be rushed.

Once you can name the manipulation — "oh, this is just urgency plus fear" — the message loses its grip. That's the actual defense, more than any spam filter.

ScamSathi breaks down the psychology behind every flagged message, not just the risk score, so the pattern sticks the next time too.
